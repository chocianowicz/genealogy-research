# Share safely: checklist

Run through this before sending a report, tree, scan or any excerpt outside your own machine
(public AI tools, forums, archives' contact forms, relatives' group chats, a public repository).

## People

- [ ] Every person who may be alive is removed or reduced to a role ("a cousin", "the owner of the house").
- [ ] No exact birth dates, addresses, phone numbers, e-mails, document numbers or national ID numbers of
      living or possibly living people. When in doubt, treat a person born in the last 100 years as living.
- [ ] No sensitive details (health, adoption, parentage surprises, DNA matches) unless the person agreed.

## Content

- [ ] Names of your own family are replaced by placeholders in anything that goes into a public place.
- [ ] Scans contain nothing beyond the record you mean to show (crop neighbouring entries, stamps with
      personal numbers, margin notes).
- [ ] Quotations are exact; nothing is paraphrased as if it were a quotation.
- [ ] Contradictions and failed searches stay in the report; do not trim them to look more certain.

## Repository

- [ ] `python3 scripts/privacy_audit.py --history` reports 0 findings with your private denylist loaded.
- [ ] Case folders (`cases/`), scans and registers are outside the repository or git-ignored.
- [ ] Archive requests are drafts; you send them yourself.
