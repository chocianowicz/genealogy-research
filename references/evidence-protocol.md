# Evidence protocol

## Artifact assessment (fill for every artifact in `artifacts.csv`)
- **Origin:** repository, collection/fond, shelfmark (ф./op./d./l. or F43-xxxx, DGS + image), URL, access date.
- **Kind:** `original` (scan of the record) · `contemporary-copy` · `later-copy` · `index` · `published`
  (critical edition, book) · `derivative` (article, tree, forum) · `oral` (family tradition).
- **Directness:** does it state the fact itself (direct) or only allow an inference (indirect)?
- **Informant:** who could know (priest at the baptism vs. revision clerk copying an older list).
- **Legibility / my reading quality:** `clear` · `partly` · `doubtful`; was it read at full resolution?
- **Independence group:** same ID for all copies/indexes/citations of one underlying record.

## Claim grading
1. List artifacts supporting and contradicting the claim.
2. Collapse artifacts into independence groups.
3. Status: see SKILL.md §1. Count *groups*, not artifacts.
4. A claim built on a chain (A is son of B, B is son of C) is no stronger than its weakest edge.

## Identity resolution
Same person only if at least one distinguishing link agrees and nothing contradicts:
- father's/husband's name; household composition across two lists; same yard/land unit (włoka, sznur, двор);
- same owner/estate and village at overlapping dates; consistent age arithmetic (± 3 years is normal in revisions);
- same parish/confession; same social estate (szlachta, boyar, peasant, townsman).
Otherwise keep separate IDs and write the candidate link as a `hypothesis`.

## Typical traps in this region
- Revision ages drift; ages in 1795 are often rounded.
- Patronymic-like surnames (‑owicz/‑evich) may still be patronymics in XVI–XVII c. documents.
- Estates change owners; the same village appears under different owners in different revisions.
- Two villages with the same name in neighbouring districts; "Wielkie/Małe", "Старые/Новые" pairs.
- Uniate → Orthodox (1839) and Catholic ↔ Orthodox conversions change where the record is.
- Published pedigrees and researchers' extracts are `published`/`researcher-conclusion` until the cited
  original is seen.
- Forum transcriptions frequently normalize spelling — always check the scan.

## Contradictions
Never resolve silently. Write: claim, conflicting artifacts, possible explanations (clerical error, two people,
date style Julian/Gregorian, copying error), what record would decide it.

## Report language
- "Established" = corroborated or better, every edge.
- "Probable" = single-source on a direct original, no contradiction.
- "Possible" = hypothesis consistent with records.
- "Unknown" = not searched or not found (say which).
