# Reading old documents

## Procedure
1. Identify language, script, document type, date, and layout (columns: names | land | dues).
2. Read at full resolution; crop and zoom to lines. If only downsized images are available, mark readings provisional.
3. Transcribe diplomatically: keep spelling, abbreviations, line breaks; expand abbreviations in [brackets];
   unclear letters as `[?]`, illegible as `[...]`; alternative readings as `Hryśko / Chryśko?`.
4. Translate separately. Then extract a name list with positions (page, line) and dues/land.
5. Cross-check every surname hit against the name-variant matrix; compare with the same estate's earlier and
   later inventories (same households usually recur).

## Polish secretary hand (XVII–XVIII c.)
- Long ſ, `cz/sz/rz` ligatures, `ẏ` for y, w/v, superscript abbreviations: `JMP` (Jego Mość Pan), `WJMP`,
  `zł` złoty, `gr` grosz, `wł.` włoka, `9bris` = Novembris, `7bris` = Septembris, `8bris`, `Xbris`.
- Formulas: "Inwentarz włości …", "sioło", "osiadły", "pusty", "przyjemny" (rented plot), "czynsz", "dziakło",
  "pańszczyzna", "tenże" (the same person), "wdowa", "sierota".
## Ruthenian / old Belarusian Cyrillic (XVI–XVII c.)
- Titlos (abbreviation marks), superscript letters, ѣ, ѧ, ѡ, ѵ; numerals as letters with titlo
  (а=1, в=2, г=3, д=4, є=5, ѕ=6, з=7, и=8, ѳ=9, і=10, к=20, л=30, м=40, н=50…).
## Russian pre-1918
- ѣ, і, ѳ, ъ; revision headings "Ревизская сказка … года … дня … о состоящих мужеска и женска пола душах";
  columns: № двора, мужской пол (по прежней ревизии / ныне), женский пол.
## Latin metric books
- Baptizavi infantem nomine …, filium/filiam legitimum, patrinis …; genitive forms; dates in Latin month names.

## Dates
- Julian vs Gregorian (Orthodox/Uniate vs Catholic; Russian civil documents are Julian until 1918).
- Inventory dates may be the date of compilation, not of the state described.
